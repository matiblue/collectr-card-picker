# Privacy Policy: Card Picker for Collectr

_Last updated: November 15, 2026 (v1.1.0)_

Card Picker for Collectr is a browser extension that lets you select cards on Collectr showcase pages and copy a list of them.

## What data the extension handles
When you click a card on `app.getcollectr.com`, the extension reads that card's publicly displayed details (name, set, card number, condition, finish, price, quantity and image URL) and adds them to your want list.

If you use **Import list**, the extension reads the text you paste or the file you choose (for example, a list of cards you still need) so it can highlight matching cards. It reads only the card name, number, set, rarity and price columns.

## Where it is stored
Your want lists, imported needs list and settings are saved only in your own browser using Chrome's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`). They never leave your device unless you choose to paste the copied list somewhere yourself.

## What we do not do
- We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.
- The extension makes no network requests of its own and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising.
- It does not read any website other than `app.getcollectr.com`.
- It does not access your Collectr account, credentials, browsing history or any personal information.

## Removing your data
Click **Clear** in the panel to empty a want list, or **Remove** to delete your imported needs list. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its stored data.
